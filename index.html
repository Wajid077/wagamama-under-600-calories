Wagamama under 600 calories

The lightest mains on the UK menu, the sides worth adding, and a few combos that keep the whole meal under 800 kcal. Figures from Wagamama's published nutrition data, November 2026.

Most Wagamama mains land between 650 and 1,000 kcal, and the katsu curries push well past that. Only five dishes on the current UK menu come in under 600. Two of them are ramen, and three are lunch wraps, so if you're eating in the evening your shortlist is short.

Watch the salt on the ramen. Both light ramen bowls go over the NHS adult limit of 6g of salt a day in a single bowl, mostly from the broth. Leave some of the broth and you cut that a lot. The wraps sit at just 1.2g.
